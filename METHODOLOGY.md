# Classification methodology

## Purpose

The classifier provides transparent preliminary decision support. It does not issue a legal opinion, conformity assessment, certification or authority determination.

## Decision order

1. Territorial scope and potential Article 2 exclusions.
2. AI-system definition under Article 3(1).
3. Operator role and GPAI position.
4. Prohibited practices under Article 5.
5. Article 6(1)/Annex I product route.
6. Article 6(2)/Annex III use-case route and Article 6(3) exception screen.
7. Article 50 transparency obligations and GPAI findings.

Potential prohibited practices take precedence in the displayed result. High-risk and transparency findings can coexist. Article 50 and GPAI findings are preserved even when another category is primary.

## Confidence

Confidence describes completeness of the supplied facts, not legal certainty. Missing intended-purpose detail, unresolved operator role, unresolved AI-system definition and incomplete Annex I/III analysis reduce confidence.

## Limits

The tool does not interpret national law, sector rules, contracts, individual authority decisions or every exception. Users must validate the actual technical design, intended purpose, affected people, deployment context and consolidated legal text.

Regulatory baseline reviewed 16 August 2026.
